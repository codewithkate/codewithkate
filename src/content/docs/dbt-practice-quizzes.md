## Prerequisites

I completed the [dbt Fundamentals](docs/dbt-learn-fundamentals.md) course around January 2026. For a few months after that I focused on data modeling and sql. This was mostly in preparation for the data engineering school interview. I completed DES training in May. 

By mid-jun, I had completed [[dbt-learn|dbt dveloper learning path]]. The courses are hands-on, but as mentioned in the dbt forums:

> The missing piece is still cohesion and guidance for everyday practitioners to help develop their testing framework.

I'd highly recommend finding your own data and following along with the course. Don't be afraid to skip around to what you need. I found out-of-order worked better than trudging through each module. Guess that's why I play more open world games than linear.

## Practice Exams
***By Qana Labs***

The week leading up to the June 18th test date was spent doing 4/11 of the Qana Labs practice exams: https://www.qanalabs.com/courses/take/dbt-developer/

>[!note] Notes on Qana Quizzes
>- Question can be repeated within the same quiz. This is helpful for recall but misleading in final scoring.
>- Answers rely on verbatim references to documentation and guides that are not always included in the official study guide.
>- Will the actual exam reference multiple datawarehouses? (Snowflake and Redshift and \<other data platform\>)

I wish I had time to do more, but I wanted to attempt the actual exam before the long holiday. At this point, I was also beginning ot burn out and wanted real feedback after increasing my practice scores from 50% to 70%. The actual exam requires a 65% to pass.
